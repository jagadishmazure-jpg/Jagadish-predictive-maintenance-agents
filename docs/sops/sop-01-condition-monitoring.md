# SOP-01: Condition Monitoring

## Vibration trending
Collect vibration readings (mm/s RMS) on rotating equipment at least daily.
Plot the 14-day trend; a sustained upward slope above 0.02 mm/s/day triggers
a watch (T2). Rule FM-VIB-01: any single reading at or above 4.5 mm/s is an
immediate alert (T3) — schedule inspection within 24 hours.

## Temperature trending
Bearing temperatures are logged alongside vibration. A 14-day slope above
0.2 C/day is a watch. Rule FM-TEMP-01: any reading at or above 85 C is an
immediate alert — the asset is stopped and inspected before restart.

## Sensor hygiene
Check sensor mounting monthly; a loose sensor reads high and mimics a
bearing fault. Re-baseline after any maintenance action so trends restart
from a known-good point.
