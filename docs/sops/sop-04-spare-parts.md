# SOP-04: Spare Parts

## Reorder discipline
Any part below its reorder point is flagged for purchase before the next
planning meeting. Lead times are tracked per part; a work order on an asset
whose parts are below reorder must note the lead-time risk.

## Kitting
P1 work orders are kitted (all parts staged) before the maintenance window.
Starting a window without staged parts is a planning miss, recorded as such.

## Vendor advisories
Review vendor advisories weekly. An advisory that matches an asset type on
site is attached to the asset's record and surfaced in the next planning
review.
